# Documentary 3D Movie-Style Reel Prompt Kit

A reusable prompt kit for making **vertical 9:16 Reels / Shorts** in a cinematic, documentary-meets-3D-animated-film style.
Use it with AI video tools (Google Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway, Hailuo, Pika, Luma) and AI script tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini).

How it works:
1. **Step 1:** Paste the **Master Reel Prompt** into a chat AI, fill the `[brackets]`. It writes the full reel plan.
2. **Step 2:** Copy each **Shot Prompt** it gives you into your video tool, one clip at a time.
3. **Step 3:** Add the voiceover, music and captions in CapCut, InShot or Instagram's editor.

A ready-made example for **Prime Growth 360 CRM** is at the end.

---

## 1. Master Reel Prompt (paste into Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini)

```
You are an award-winning documentary director and 3D animation film director.
Create a 30–45 second vertical (9:16) Instagram Reel / YouTube Short in a
"documentary 3D movie" style: the emotional truth of a real documentary,
shot like a premium 3D animated feature film (Pixar / DreamWorks-level
lighting and character work, but original characters and settings).

TOPIC: [what the reel is about]
BRAND / CREATOR: [name]
AUDIENCE: [who should watch, e.g. small shop owners in India]
MAIN CHARACTER: [age, job, look, clothing, location]
PROBLEM (the struggle): [the pain point]
TURNING POINT: [the discovery / product / idea]
RESULT (the transformation): [the outcome, with a number if possible]
CALL TO ACTION: [e.g. "Follow for more", "Link in bio", "Try it free"]
LANGUAGE OF VOICEOVER: [English / Hindi / Hinglish / Gujarati / ...]
MOOD: [inspiring / emotional / hopeful / epic]

Structure the reel as:
- HOOK (0–3s): a striking visual + one line that stops the scroll
- STRUGGLE (3–12s): show the problem, the character's real life
- TURNING POINT (12–22s): the moment things change
- TRANSFORMATION (22–35s): the result, proof, emotion
- CTA (35–45s): brand + call to action

Give me:
1. A one-line concept and a title for the reel.
2. A shot list of 6–8 shots. For EACH shot give:
   - Timecode and duration (each clip 4–8 seconds)
   - A ready-to-paste AI VIDEO PROMPT (single paragraph, 60–100 words) that
     includes: subject + action, setting, camera move, lens, lighting,
     colour grade, mood, "3D animated documentary film style, 9:16 vertical,
     cinematic, highly detailed, 4K"
   - On-screen text (max 6 words)
   - Voiceover line for that shot
   - Sound effect / ambience
3. The full voiceover script (documentary narrator tone: calm, deep, warm).
4. Music direction (genre, BPM, where the beat drops).
5. Caption (2–3 lines), 10 hashtags, and a thumbnail/cover prompt.
6. A consistent CHARACTER DESCRIPTION paragraph I can reuse in every shot
   prompt so the character looks the same in every clip.

Rules: keep the character, outfit and colour palette identical across shots;
no real people, logos or copyrighted characters; one clear action per shot.
```

## 2. Style Block (add to the end of every video prompt)

```
3D animated documentary film style, photoreal-stylised 3D characters,
cinematic lighting, soft volumetric light rays, shallow depth of field,
anamorphic lens look, subtle film grain, warm teal-and-amber colour grade,
realistic textures, expressive faces, slow deliberate camera movement,
vertical 9:16 frame, 4K, highly detailed, emotional storytelling
```

### Negative prompt (for tools that support it)
```
blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, text
artifacts, watermark, logo, cartoonish flat 2D, oversaturated, jittery motion,
morphing, duplicate characters, horizontal frame
```

## 3. Camera & Lighting Cheat Sheet

| Purpose | Use in prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Hook / epic open | "slow aerial drone push-in over the city at golden hour" |
| Intimacy / emotion | "close-up, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, eyes in focus" |
| Struggle / pressure | "handheld documentary camera, slight shake, cool blue light, rain on window" |
| Reveal / turning point | "slow dolly-in, light bursts through the window, dust particles glowing" |
| Success / transformation | "low-angle hero shot, warm golden backlight, slow-motion" |
| Data / product | "macro shot of glowing holographic screen, floating UI particles" |
| Ending / CTA | "slow pull-back crane shot revealing the full scene, sunset" |

**Documentary touches:** archival-style title cards ("Surat, 2026"), lower-third name tags, interview-style framing (character looking slightly off-camera), natural ambience.

## 5. Ready Example: Prime Growth 360 CRM Reel

**Title:** "The Notebook That Almost Closed His Shop"
**Concept:** A small-shop owner loses customers to forgotten follow-ups, then runs his whole business from one free CRM.
**Length:** 40 seconds · 9:16 · Voiceover in Hinglish (or English)

### Character (reuse in every shot)
```
Raj, a 38-year-old Indian shop owner, short black hair, light stubble,
wearing a mustard-yellow kurta and black-framed glasses, kind tired eyes,
3D animated film character
```

### Shot list

**Shot 1 — HOOK (0–4s)**
- Prompt: `Slow aerial drone push-in over a busy Indian market street at dawn, hundreds of small shops opening their shutters, golden mist, birds flying, then the camera dives toward one small electronics shop with a faded sign. 3D animated documentary film style, cinematic lighting, volumetric light rays, warm teal-and-amber grade, vertical 9:16, 4K, highly detailed.`
- Text: **"63 million small businesses."**
- VO: "India has sixty-three million small businesses. This is the story of one."
- SFX: market ambience, distant temple bell

**Shot 2 — STRUGGLE (4–10s)**
- Prompt: `[Character] sits behind a cluttered shop counter at night, flipping through a torn paper notebook full of crossed-out phone numbers, receipts falling to the floor, a single tube light flickering above. Handheld documentary camera, slight shake, medium close-up, 50mm lens, cool blue light with harsh shadows. [Style block]`
- Text: **"Every lead in one notebook."**
- VO: "Raj kept every customer, every payment, every promise… in one notebook."

**Shot 3 — STRUGGLE (10–15s)**
- Prompt: `Close-up of [character]'s worried face lit by his phone screen showing a missed message: "Bought from another shop. Thanks." Rain streaks down the shop window behind him. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, eyes in focus, cold blue tones. [Style block]`
- Text: **"Forgotten follow-up = lost sale"**
- VO: "One forgotten call. One lost customer. Then another."
- SFX: rain, phone buzz

**Shot 4 — TURNING POINT (15–21s)**
- Prompt: `[Character] opens a laptop on the counter; a soft green-and-white glowing dashboard lights up his face, floating holographic cards of contacts, follow-ups and invoices rise gently from the screen like particles. Slow dolly-in, morning light bursts through the shop shutter, glowing dust particles. [Style block]`
- Text: **"Then he found Prime Growth 360"**
- VO: "Then he moved his whole shop into one free CRM."
- Music: beat drop here

**Shot 5 — TRANSFORMATION (21–27s)**
- Prompt: `Montage-style shot: [character] taps "WhatsApp" on a follow-up reminder, a customer at the door smiles and walks in, a GST invoice prints from a small printer, the sales pipeline cards slide from "Lead" to "Won". Smooth gimbal tracking shot, bright warm daylight, lively shop. [Style block]`
- Text: **"Follow-ups. Invoices. Pipeline."**
- VO: "Reminders on time. GST invoices in seconds. Every deal in one view."

**Shot 6 — RESULT (27–33s)**
- Prompt: `Low-angle hero shot of [character] standing proudly in front of his renovated, busy shop with a fresh bright sign, customers inside, warm golden backlight, slow motion, confetti-like light particles. [Style block]`
- Text: **"Sales up. Stress down."**
- VO: "Same shop. Same Raj. A completely different business."

**Shot 7 — CTA (33–40s)**
- Prompt: `Slow crane pull-back from the shop revealing the whole glowing market street at sunset, hundreds of shops lighting up one by one like a network of connected lights. [Style block]`
- Text: **"Prime Growth 360 — Free CRM. Link in bio."**
- VO: "Your business deserves more than a notebook. Prime Growth 360. Free. Start today."

### Music
Cinematic emotional piano opening (70 BPM), builds with strings, beat drop into an uplifting Indian-fusion percussion track at 15s.

### Caption & hashtags
> He almost lost his shop to a notebook. 📒➡️📈
> Prime Growth 360 is a free CRM for small businesses: contacts, follow-ups, GST invoices, pipeline. Link in bio.

`#smallbusiness #msme #crm #businessgrowth #indianbusiness #entrepreneur #gstinvoice #shopowner #documentary #reelsindia`

### Thumbnail / cover prompt
```
[Character] holding a torn notebook in one hand and a glowing laptop with a
dashboard in the other, split lighting (cold blue left, warm gold right),
dramatic 3D animated film poster style, vertical 9:16, bold empty space at
top for title text, 4K
```

## 6. Quick Tips for Better Reels

- **Consistency:** paste the same character paragraph into every shot; use the tool's "character reference" / "image-to-video" with a first generated still.
- **Clip length:** generate 5–8 s clips; trim to the best 3–5 s in editing.
- **Hook first:** the first 2 seconds decide the reel. Movement + bold text + a question or a number.
- **Captions:** always burn in subtitles; most people watch on mute.
- **Voiceover:** use ElevenLabs or CapCut text-to-speech with a "deep documentary narrator" voice, or record your own.
- **Series idea:** reuse this kit for a weekly "MSME Stories" documentary series: clinic, coaching centre, manufacturer, trader, real-estate agent.
