# Project rules

## Reel / video editing requests (standing rule from the owner)
Whenever a video or reel edit is requested:
1. Show the user's own video in the right-side panel as soon as it is ready (SendUserFile, display "render"), starting with the cleaned raw clip.
2. Build and publish a complete, editable video-editing timeline as an Artifact in the panel, made to look like a professional editor (NLE). It must include:
   - all clips in sequence with exact start/end times
   - Gujarati subtitle track (plus English subtitle track when used)
   - voice-over/audio track with waveform
   - background music track
   - SFX track
   - transitions
   - scene-by-scene editing instructions
   - timeline ruler and a draggable playhead synced to a preview monitor
   Not just a frame analyzer or a static storyboard.
3. Keep the timeline updated (same artifact URL) whenever the edit plan changes, and send the final MP4 to the panel too.
