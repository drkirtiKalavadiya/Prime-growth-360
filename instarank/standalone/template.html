Which Reels are winning?

Search a keyword on Instagram, open the Reels tab, and note each top Reel's likes, comments and age. Paste one Reel per line as likes | comments | age | caption. Age can be 45m, 6h or 3d. Paste the same Reels again later and the analyzer shows engagement gained per minute.
