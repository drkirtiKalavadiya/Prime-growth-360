## How it works and what you need
The skill runs inside Claude Code on your computer. You need your own Claude subscription that includes Claude Code,
Python, Node.js and ffmpeg (the setup check tells you what is missing). Results depend on your clip (framing, light,
background) and on the Claude model you use; follow the Guide for best results.
