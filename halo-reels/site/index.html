How it works

You direct. Claude edits.

Approve the stills

Claude cuts you out, finds every word, plans five scenes and shows one still per scene. Change anything in one sentence.

Post the reel

Claude renders the finished MP4 with motion blur, transitions and sound, mixed for Instagram. Done.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Claude Code is only the name of the mode. You write to Claude in normal sentences, like “make my room brighter”.

Do I need Premiere or CapCut?

No. You get a finished MP4 with your voice and the sound design. Open it in any editor afterwards if you want music or a longer edit.

How long does one reel take?

Stills in about a minute. The final 10–15 second reel in about 6–12 minutes on a normal laptop, while you do something else.

Which languages work?

English and the Latin-script languages: Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and more.

What kind of clip works best?

Vertical, on a tripod, seated or standing, chest-up, with a calm room behind you and soft front light. The Guide shows what to do with close-ups, horizontal clips and walking shots.

Can I use it for clients?

Yes. The reels are yours, for your own content and your clients’. The skill itself is licensed to one person and can’t be shared or resold.
