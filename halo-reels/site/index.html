Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You write to Claude in normal sentences, like “make my room brighter”.

What do I need?

A Claude plan with Claude Code (desktop app or terminal), and a Mac, Windows or Linux computer. The setup check installs Python, Node.js and ffmpeg with you.

How long does one reel take?

Stills in about a minute. The final 10–15 second reel in about 6–12 minutes on a normal laptop.

Do I need Premiere or CapCut?

No. You get a finished MP4 with your voice and sound design.

Which languages work?

English and the Latin-script languages: Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and more.

Can I use it for clients?

Yes, the reels are yours. The skill is licensed to one person and can’t be shared or resold.
